# Idle Realms

An idle fantasy RPG for Android. Train your skills by chopping, mining, fishing and crafting your own weapons and armor, then take your character into arena battles and monster hunts. Unlock new looks, complete quests and tasks, and keep progressing while the app is closed.

## How to install
1. On your Android phone, open this repository's **Releases** page and download `IdleRealms.apk` from the latest release.
2. Open the downloaded file. The first time, Android asks you to allow installs from that app (for example Files or Chrome). Allow it.
3. Tap **Install**, then open **Idle Realms**.

New versions install over the old one, and your progress is kept.
